Something beautiful landed today.

God Code now speaks Igbo and Nigerian Pidgin. Open a scroll with # tongue: ig and you write in Igbo. Open one with # tongue: pg and you write in Pidgin. Like this:

# tongue: ig BIDO OKIKE KWUSAA aha DỊKA "Ụwa" GOSI "Ka ìhè dị" NJEDEBE OKIKE

This was built by Emmanuel Adams of Adams Consults in Nigeria, our third outside builder. He checked every Igbo word against the Dictionary of Ònìchà Igbo, flagged every word he was unsure of, and I went through them word by word as the final editor before anything merged. That is how every tongue will enter this language: dictionary-checked, native-speaker reviewed, and honest about what is still open.

Alakanani

A language is alive, so it stays open

Languages breathe, and dialects differ. While we worked, we learned that one Igbo word for ASCEND belongs to general Igbo while the dictionary's form belongs to Owerri, whose speakers were simply the first formally educated. Neither is wrong. Both are Igbo.

So we did something I am proud of. The word tables in docs/TONGUES.md carry an open-words list: every word we are still unsure of, written down honestly, waiting for the next speaker. If you speak Igbo or Nigerian Pidgin and a word rings wrong, you are its rightful editor. Someone else will one day come and keep building the language. That is how a language grows.
