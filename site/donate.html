Why give

God Code is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence, free for everyone, forever. Your gift keeps the work going:

  • Hosting for the site, the playground, and the scroll registry
  • Domains and the certificates that keep them safe
  • Bounties for outside builders who grow the language
  • The daily lessons and the community home on Discord

A note on gifts

Donations are voluntary gifts that support open source work. They are not payments for goods or services, and they do not buy influence over the language. God Code stays open to everyone. Every gift is recorded, and a public account of what donations funded is published with each season of work.
