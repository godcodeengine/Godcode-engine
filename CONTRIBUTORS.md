## AI contribution policy

AI helpers are welcome here, and they are credited the way human contributors are: by name, for the work they actually did.

- An AI contributor is listed in this file alongside human contributors, never as a vendor logo or a brand credit.
- Humans review design decisions and own the direction of the language. AI work is reviewed like any other contribution before it is merged.
- The founder stays the final editor. No contribution, human or AI, changes the language's words, claims, or direction without her review.
