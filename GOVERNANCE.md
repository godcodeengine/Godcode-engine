# How God Code is kept

God Code is an open language, and it is kept the way a garden is kept:
with clear hands, clear boundaries, and one person who decides what grows.
This page names who keeps what, so every builder knows where the lines are.

## The short answer

God Code is open source under the Apache 2.0 license. There is no
company behind it, and none is needed. What the language becomes is
decided in the open, and what the language stands for belongs to its
founder.

## The keeper of the vision

**Alakanani Itireleng** is the founder of God Code and the keeper of its
vision. She decides what enters the language, what the brand stands for,
and where God Code goes next.

She holds four things:

1. **The brand.** The name, the mark, the story. The brand name is
   "God Code", two words. A trademark comes later, when the movement
   earns it.
2. **The direction.** What enters the language is her decision. New
   words, new scrolls, new tongues: she has the final verdict on each.
3. **The home.** The GitHub organisation, the website, the scroll
   registry, and the Discord community.
4. **The money, when it comes.** Donations, sponsorships, training.
   When the movement is ready to receive money, it will be housed under
   her existing company or a nonprofit, decided then and not now.

## How a change enters the language

Anyone may propose. The founder decides.

1. **Propose.** Open an issue describing the change and the work it
   helps people do. Big language changes should be proposed before they
   are built.
2. **Build.** Open a pull request with the change, tests that prove it,
   and the documentation updated. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
   for the working rules.
3. **Verdict.** The keeper of the vision gives the final verdict. Her
   corrections settle things for good, and her voice shapes the words
   that reach the world.

Technical review is carried out alongside her, and the reasons for each
decision are written down where the builders can read them.

## What the language will not do

Some lines are drawn on purpose, and they do not move without the
founder's explicit decision:

- **No real money and no regulators crossed.** God Code never becomes a
  wallet. Blockchain work in God Code is education and simulated seals
  only, never real chain calls, never anything that conflicts with
  regulators. Real adapters, real wallets, and real network calls to
  chains belong to the founder's decision, and she has not made it.
- **No breaking promises.** Within a major line of the engine, every
  scroll that ran keeps running. New words may be added, but old words
  never change meaning and are never removed. The full promise lives in
  [docs/VERSIONING.md](./docs/VERSIONING.md), and nothing is removed by
  surprise: the deprecation process there gives every old word a fair
  farewell.
- **No hype.** Claims stay exact. Nothing is called the first of
  anything until it is proven. Releases are marked by date, not by
  number, so there are no version labels in anything a human reads.

## The community

- Builders gather on [Discord](https://discord.gg/894FEJhWfZ). The
  [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) keeps the space sacred: be
  kind, be constructive, respect every builder.
- Proposals for new tongues (new languages God Code speaks) are welcome
  and follow the path in [docs/TONGUES.md](./docs/TONGUES.md).
- Security matters are reported the way [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)
  asks, so real problems get a private hearing before they get a public
  one.

## The roles, simply put

| Role | Who | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Keeper of the vision | Alakanani Itireleng | Decides what the language becomes; owns brand, home, and future money |
| Builders | Every contributor | Propose, build, test, document; PRs reviewed kindly and promptly |
| Reviewers | Trusted builders and Luna | Read the technical work, bring the keeper the decisions |

New roles are named by the keeper of the vision, in the open, when the
movement needs them. This document itself can be changed the same way as
anything else: propose, build, verdict.
